- How to Use These Papers with Your Daughter

  • Start with Summer Test 1 today. Do it together once so she understands the format. Then she can do them independently.
  • One test per week. 10 questions takes about 15 minutes. Mark it together and celebrate every improvement.
  • Keep a simple score tracker. Write the date, test number, and score out of 10. Seeing the line go up is hugely motivating.
  • Use online games between tests. Topmarks or Mathsframe for 5-10 minutes on non-test days keeps skills sharp.
  • Times tables are the foundation. Year 4 pupils sit the MTC. Mathsframe's MTC simulator is the best free prep tool.
  • If she struggles with a topic, use the Third Space Learning worksheets to target that area before the next test.
